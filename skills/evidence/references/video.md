# Recordings

- Prefer computer use for UI recordings when available.
- Record at a natural, unhurried pace, as a user would. Pause long enough to read important fields and results.
- Keep the pointer visible and move it deliberately to controls before clicking. Avoid sudden jumps and unnecessary movement.
- Record only the visible viewport. Scroll naturally to reach other areas; do not use full-page captures.
- Show the before state, action, and actual result. Keep one case per clip and use normal playback speed.
- No overlays, captions, or added text are needed.
- Check that the video plays clearly and inspect frames for secrets and unrelated personal data before delivery.
